History of Brass

------------------------------------------------◆------------------------------------------------

"From ancient horns to modern masterpieces, brass instruments have shaped music, culture and ceremony for thousands of years."

A Journey Through Time

------------------------------------------------◆------------------------------------------------

Ancient Beginnings

Before 1500 BC

Early brass instruments were simple natural horns made from animal horns, shells, or metal. They were used in rituals, communication and to announce important events.

The Medieval & Renaissance Era

500 – 1600

Brass instruments became more refined and were used in royal courts, military bands and religious ceremonies. The sackbut and natural trumpet were popular during this period.

The Classical Period

1600 – 1800

Composers began writing music specifically for brass instruments. New developments in instrument design expanded their range and expressive possibilities.

The Modern Era

1800 – Present

Brass instruments continued to evolve with new designs, materials and technology. Today, they are found in orchestras, concert bands, jazz, popular music and many other styles.
